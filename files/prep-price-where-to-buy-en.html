PrEP in Thailand: Price, Where to Get It and Online PrEP with Nationwide Delivery

Monthly cost, every way to get PrEP in Thailand (free and paid), and how online PrEP with a doctor works, from consultation and blood tests to discreet delivery.

Want to start PrEP without queuing at a hospital? Where can you get it in Thailand, and how much does it cost each month?

One pill a day prevents HIV very effectively, but it has to be started properly. This page brings together prices, where to get PrEP and how online PrEP with a doctor works.

Quick summary

  • PrEP at our clinic starts at 1,290 THB for 30 tablets (Teno-EM), about 43 THB a day. If a doctor confirms 2-1-1 dosing suits you, each episode uses 4 tablets, about 172 THB.*
  • Thai nationals can get PrEP free under the national health scheme at participating services. Foreign nationals and tourists usually pay. (Free vs clinic PrEP compared)
  • A clinic suits people who want privacy, flexible times, care while travelling, or who have no Thai health coverage.
  • Online PrEP: consult a doctor on LINE → blood tests → prescription → discreet delivery anywhere in Thailand, with an HIV test every 3 months.
  • Never start PrEP without an HIV test first. Taking it with an undiagnosed infection can cause drug resistance.

PrEP (pre-exposure prophylaxis) is an HIV medicine taken before possible exposure to prevent infection. Taken consistently, it lowers the risk of getting HIV from sex by about 99%, according to the CDC. Most people want three answers before starting: what it costs, where to get it, and whether they can get it without going to a hospital. (Basics: What is PrEP and who is it for)

Where we stand on price: at our clinic TAF-EM (TAF/FTC) costs the same as Teno-EM, 1,290 THB, while TAF-based PrEP elsewhere in Thailand is commonly around 1,500–1,800 THB a month. TAF is gentler on kidneys and bones, useful for long-term users when the doctor confirms it suits them.

Is 2-1-1 cheaper? If you have sex less often and a doctor confirms it suits you (TDF/FTC only, for men with anal-sex exposure), each episode uses 4 tablets. One bottle covers about 7 episodes, roughly 172 THB each. See On-demand PrEP (2-1-1).

Total cost to expect: besides the medicine, there are baseline blood tests (HIV, kidney function, hepatitis B), a doctor’s fee and 120 THB delivery. Our staff confirm the full amount before anything is charged, with no hidden costs.

Who should consider PrEP?

PrEP is for anyone who may be exposed to HIV. Our doctor discusses it without judgment and keeps everything confidential.

Men who have sex with menSeveral partners, or condoms not every time
Trans womenPrEP can be taken with hormones; the doctor chooses the regimen and follows up
Sex workersOngoing, friendly protection for higher-risk work (read more)
Serodiscordant couplesA partner with HIV who is not yet undetectable (U=U)
Recent PEP or STIRepeated PEP use, or syphilis or gonorrhoea in the past year
ChemsexDrugs make protection harder (harm reduction)

Tourists, expats and people working abroad

Visitors to Koh Samui, Koh Phangan, Phuket or Bangkok usually cannot use Thailand’s free PrEP programme. You can consult our doctor in English, do the baseline tests locally, and have PrEP delivered to your hotel or address in Thailand. If you are travelling on, plan ahead: the doctor may consider a multi-month supply where appropriate.

  • Before you fly: test and collect your supply in Thailand.
  • Keep in touch on LINE for questions on missed doses, side effects or a possible exposure needing PEP.
  • Delivery is within Thailand only.

Also see Travel health checklist.

How to take PrEP and when it protects you

  • Daily: one tablet at the same time each day, suitable for everyone. Full protection for anal sex after about 7 days, for vaginal sex after about 21 days.
  • 2-1-1: 2 tablets 2–24 hours before sex, then 1 tablet 24 and 48 hours after the first dose. TDF/FTC only, for men with anal-sex exposure, after a doctor’s assessment.
  • PrEP does not prevent syphilis, gonorrhoea or chlamydia. Use condoms and test for STIs every 3 months; some people may benefit from Doxy-PEP.
  • If you had a risk in the last 72 hours and were not on PrEP, you need PEP, not PrEP (what to do after an exposure).

Long-acting injectable PrEP: see Cabotegravir and lenacapavir in Thailand.

Why not just buy PrEP without a doctor?

  • Drug resistance: if you already have HIV without knowing it, PrEP (only two drugs) can lead to resistant virus, so an HIV test is required first.
  • Kidneys and bones: TDF needs kidney checks before starting and during use.
  • Hepatitis B: stopping PrEP on your own can trigger a hepatitis flare.
  • Fake or poorly stored medicine from unreliable sellers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PrEP cost in Thailand?

At Dr. Kris Clinic, Teno-EM and TAF-EM cost 1,290 THB for 30 tablets and Ricovir-EM 1,590 THB. Blood tests, the doctor’s fee and 120 THB delivery are extra. Prices may change; please confirm with our staff on LINE.

Where can I get PrEP in Thailand?

Public hospitals (free for Thai nationals), community and key-population clinics, private clinics and doctor telemedicine services. Avoid sellers who do not test for HIV first.

Can tourists get PrEP in Thailand?

Yes. Tourists and expats usually pay, as the free programme is for Thai nationals. You can consult our doctor in English and receive PrEP at your hotel or address in Thailand after testing.

Can I buy PrEP online?

We do not sell medicines online. Every patient consults a doctor on LINE or by telemedicine and has blood tests first. After a prescription we deliver discreetly anywhere in Thailand.

Do you deliver PrEP to Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Phuket?

Yes, we deliver anywhere in Thailand in plain packaging. Our clinic is in Surat Thani, close to Samui and Phangan, if you prefer to visit.

What tests do I need before PrEP?

An HIV test to confirm you are negative, kidney function and hepatitis B, then an HIV test every 3 months while on PrEP.

Can I get PrEP for a trip abroad?

Consult and test before you leave. The doctor may consider a supply for several months where appropriate. Delivery is within Thailand only.

What is the difference between Teno-EM and TAF-EM?

Both prevent HIV well. Teno-EM (TDF/FTC) can be used daily or 2-1-1. TAF-EM (TAF/FTC) is daily only and gentler on kidneys and bones, but is not recommended for vaginal-sex exposure.

Why every hour counts

PEP is a short course of HIV medicine taken after a possible exposure, to stop the virus from taking hold. CDC (2025) and WHO (2024) guidelines say to start as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours and no later than 72 hours. After 72 hours it is no longer recommended. The course lasts 28 days.

0–24 hBest window
24–72 hStill possible, sooner is better
After 72 hPEP not recommended

Common situations: a condom broke or slipped off, anal or vaginal sex without a condom with someone whose HIV status you do not know, or shared needles. If you were sexually assaulted, go to the nearest hospital now (One Stop Crisis Center 1300). When PEP is needed

    Frequently asked questions

    Where can I get PEP in Surat Thani, and how fast?

    In Mueang Surat Thani district, message the doctor on LINE @dr.krisclinic. Once the doctor confirms PEP is right for you, a rider delivers it to where you are staying within a few hours, every day including late at night. You can also pick it up at the clinic during opening hours.

    Do I need a blood test before PEP? Do I have to wait for the result?

    You should have an anti-HIV test before or on the day you start, but you do not need to wait for the result before the first dose. MT Lab gives fast results, or you can use a finger-prick HIV self-test delivered with your medicine.

    It is late at night and labs are closed. What now?

    Message the doctor on LINE anyway. After the assessment, your PEP is delivered together with an HIV self-test. Do the test, send a photo of the result to the doctor, and take the first dose as instructed. No need to wait for a lab to open.

    I am on Koh Samui, Koh Phangan or in another district. Can you deliver?

    Rider delivery covers Mueang Surat Thani district only. Elsewhere, come to the clinic or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Do not wait for a parcel if it would take you past 72 hours.

    How much does PEP cost?

    PEP course 1,590 THB, doctor chat consultation 390 THB, HIV self-test 250 THB (optional), rider in Mueang Surat Thani 120 THB. Estimated total 2,350 THB (or 2,100 THB plus the lab fee if you test at MT Lab instead of using the self-test). Please ask MT Lab directly for its test fee. Prices may change with drug costs; please confirm with staff.

    What do I do after finishing PEP?

    While on PEP you can ask about side effects on LINE at no extra cost. Repeat an HIV test at 4–6 weeks and 12 weeks after the exposure. If you are likely to be at risk again, talk to the doctor about PrEP, which can start straight after PEP once your test is negative.
